Students' Services

Smart Classes

At the door step of the new digital era, S K Presidency Public School has reinvented itself with smart classes. The new age Smart Class rooms are equipped with the latest digital means like computers and interactive teaching software, making lessons more enjoyable and easier to perceive for the new age learners.

The Green Digital Campus

The institute is digitally facilitated which helps in frequent update through message service to keep the students, parents and the teachers updated about studies or any other activities in the campus. Cleanliness and hygiene is strictly maintained within the campus. Use of plastic bags and other bio-degradable compounds are strictly prohibited inside the campus. Frequently placed waste bins, covered sewage, drinking water supply etc. are the other infra structural arrangements that make life at S K Presidency Public School beautiful and healthy.

Grievance Solving Unit

At S K Presidency Public School, we take utmost care of all the grievances and take into consideration your feedbacks and suggestions. We believe in providing fluidity and maximum satisfaction, so all the suggestions and grievances are directly taken care off by our management. Your words will reach the management of the S K Presidency Public School directly for quick measures. You can write to us and send it to principal@skpresidency.com or drop it in the Grievance Box at our campus.

Library

"A library is like an island in the vast sea of knowledge". SKPPS has a well stocked library with a decent collection of books on various subjects, reference books, story books (fiction and non-fiction) in English and Hindi Journal, Magazines, Sports Magazines and Newspapers. Students can avail lending and reading room facilities regularly. The digital facilities enable the students to access a large number of e-books.

Laboratories

The school offers a series of updated and dedicated laboratories for subjects like mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology and computer science. We boast our upbeat labs which provide our students with tremendous opportunities for applying creativity and innovation in the teaching and learning processes.
