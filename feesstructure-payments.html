Fees Structure & Payments

Tuition fee and transport are charged for 12 months and monthly tuition fee should be paid on or before the 10th of every month, failing which a late fine of Rs. 200/- will be charged. No reduction will be made for holidays or broken period. Tuition fees and transport fee must be paid through cheque or demand draft.
